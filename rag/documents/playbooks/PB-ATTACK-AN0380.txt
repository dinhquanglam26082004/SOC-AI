Investigation guidance:
Official analytic text:
Detects non-interactive or script-driven email transmission using tools like `sendmail`, `mailx`, or custom SMTP scripts by background processes, especially when sending attachments or large payloads.

Local applicability (SOC-AI project guidance, not MITRE text):
Use retained Wazuh authentication/FIM/sudo records and Zeek conn/HTTP/DNS only when the required source exists. These are historical observations, not live endpoint inspection. Process lineage, resource utilization, service availability and authorization may require additional sources or analyst verification.
