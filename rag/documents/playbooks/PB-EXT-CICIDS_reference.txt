PLAYBOOK ID: PB-EXT-CICIDS
NAME: CICIDS2017 and CSE-CIC-IDS2018 public dataset reference
Provenance: Curated summaries, reviewed 2026-11-02. Sources: https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2017.html and https://www.unb.ca/cic/datasets/ids-2018.html . Reference guidance only; these are historical research datasets, not events from the current SOC.
CICIDS2017 includes benign activity, SSH and FTP brute force, DoS, DDoS, web attacks, infiltration and botnet scenarios. The publisher provides packet captures and labeled flow CSV data. CSE-CIC-IDS2018 also covers brute force, DoS/DDoS, web attacks and other scenarios.
Local application guidance: use scenario descriptions to organize investigation questions. A flow label does not prove why a particular local alert fired. CICFlowMeter feature columns do not map one-to-one to Zeek conn fields or Wazuh authentication events. Network-only observations cannot establish who logged in successfully or what commands executed. Keep dataset IPs, times and labels separate from operational evidence. Consult publisher access and citation terms before downloading or redistributing raw captures. This knowledge entry contains no raw PCAP/CSV records and does not train or fine-tune Qwen3.
