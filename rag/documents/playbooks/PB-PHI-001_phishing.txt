PLAYBOOK ID: PB-PHI-001

NAME:
Phishing Email Response

TRIGGER:
User reports suspicious email, or email security gateway flags a message
as phishing / malicious attachment / credential harvesting link.

INVESTIGATION:
1. Obtain the original email headers and body (do not click any links).
2. Extract and defang all URLs and attachment hashes.
3. Check URLs against threat intelligence (VirusTotal, URLScan.io).
4. Check attachment hashes against AV and sandboxing tools.
5. Identify all recipients of the same campaign within the organization.
6. Check mail server logs for delivery and open/click events.
7. Determine whether any user clicked links or opened attachments.
8. If credentials were submitted: check for account logins from unusual IPs
   or locations in the past 24 hours.
9. Correlate with EDR telemetry for payload execution on endpoints.

RESPONSE:
1. Quarantine the phishing email from all affected mailboxes.
2. Block sender domain and all extracted malicious URLs at the gateway.
3. If a user clicked a link or opened an attachment:
   a. Isolate the endpoint from the network.
   b. Force password reset for the affected account(s).
   c. Revoke active sessions / OAuth tokens.
4. Notify affected users and their managers.
5. Submit indicators to threat intelligence platform.
6. Preserve email headers, URLs, and attachment samples as evidence.

ESCALATION:
Escalate to Tier 2 / Incident Response if:
- Credential theft is confirmed.
- Malware execution is detected on any endpoint.
- More than 10 users received or interacted with the email.

Human approval is required before quarantining mailboxes, blocking
domains, isolating endpoints, or resetting user credentials.
