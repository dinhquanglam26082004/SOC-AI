PLAYBOOK ID: PB-EXT-DOS
NAME: DoS DDoS network flooding and endpoint exhaustion investigation
Provenance: Curated paraphrases, reviewed 2026-11-02. MITRE T1498 https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1498/ and T1499 https://attack.mitre.org/techniques/T1499/ . Guidance only; not incident evidence.
Network denial of service can exhaust bandwidth through traffic from one or multiple systems. Reflection and amplification complicate source attribution. Endpoint denial of service can exhaust host or application resources without saturating the network.
Local application guidance: use search_zeek_connections to inspect affected destinations, ports, timestamps, protocol, packets, bytes and connection states. Use HTTP observations when available to investigate application requests. Compare a consistent time window against a baseline. Failed connections alone may reflect a closed port, firewall policy or an unavailable service. Many source addresses do not independently establish a coordinated DDoS. Confirm service impact with independent latency, availability, resource or service-error evidence. Current tools do not directly measure these signals; report that gap. Do not invent a universal numerical attack threshold. Recommend analyst-approved containment or upstream filtering only after assessing service impact and legitimate traffic.
