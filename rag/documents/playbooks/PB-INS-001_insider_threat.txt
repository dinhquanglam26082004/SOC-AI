PLAYBOOK ID: PB-INS-001

NAME:
Insider Threat Response

TRIGGER:
Behavioral analytics or DLP alert indicating potential insider threat:
- Employee accessing data outside normal working hours or from unusual
  location, especially before resignation/termination.
- Bulk download of sensitive files to personal device or cloud storage.
- Privilege abuse: access to data beyond job function.
- User account active after offboarding.

INVESTIGATION:
1. Engage HR and Legal BEFORE alerting or confronting the user.
   This investigation must be covert in initial stages.
2. Collect and preserve logs without modifying them:
   - VPN and authentication logs.
   - File access logs (DLP, DRM, file server audit).
   - Email logs (sent items, forwarding rules).
   - Cloud storage access (OneDrive, SharePoint, personal accounts).
   - Badge access records.
3. Establish a baseline of the user's normal behavior patterns.
4. Document all anomalous activities with timestamps.
5. Determine whether the activity is malicious, negligent, or a
   policy violation (misuse vs. deliberate theft).
6. Assess what data was accessed, copied, or transmitted.
7. Preserve chain of custody for all evidence (required for legal action).

RESPONSE:
1. All response actions require approval from HR, Legal, and Management.
2. If termination is pending or confirmed:
   a. Immediately revoke all access on the last day of employment.
   b. Disable accounts, revoke VPN certificates, remove email access.
   c. Recover company-owned devices.
3. If active data exfiltration is occurring:
   a. Block outbound transfer at the proxy (without alerting user).
   b. Preserve evidence simultaneously.
4. Conduct exit interview coordinated with HR (if appropriate).
5. File a law enforcement report if criminal activity (IP theft, fraud)
   is confirmed.
6. Review access controls to prevent similar incidents:
   - Implement need-to-know data access.
   - Enable User Behavior Analytics (UBA) monitoring.

ESCALATION:
Engage HR, Legal, and executive management at the earliest stage.
This playbook must NOT be executed by SOC Tier 1 alone.

All actions against the suspected user account require explicit written
approval from HR Director and Legal Counsel.
