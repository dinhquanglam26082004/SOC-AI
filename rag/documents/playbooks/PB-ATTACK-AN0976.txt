Investigation guidance:
Official analytic text:
Monitor audit logs for setuid/setgid bit changes, executions where UID ≠ EUID (indicative of sudo or privilege escalation), and high-integrity binaries launched by unprivileged users.

Local applicability (SOC-AI project guidance, not MITRE text):
Use retained Wazuh authentication/FIM/sudo records and Zeek conn/HTTP/DNS only when the required source exists. These are historical observations, not live endpoint inspection.
