Investigation guidance:
Official analytic text:
Abuse of extensible server modules (e.g., Apache, Nginx, Tomcat) to load rogue plugins that initiate bash, connect to C2, or spawn reverse shells.

Local applicability (SOC-AI project guidance, not MITRE text):
Use retained Wazuh authentication/FIM/sudo records and Zeek conn/HTTP/DNS only when the required source exists. These are historical observations, not live endpoint inspection. Process lineage, resource utilization, service availability and authorization may require additional sources or analyst verification. Do not claim those signals were checked unless an actual tool returned them. No numerical threshold here establishes an attack by itself.
