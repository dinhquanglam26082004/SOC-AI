SOC ALERT TRIAGE POLICY

PURPOSE:
Define the process for SOC analysts to consistently triage, classify,
and prioritize incoming alerts.

TRIAGE STEPS:

Step 1 — Validate the Alert:
- Confirm the alert is not a known false positive from the false
  positive register.
- Verify the alert data (IP, hostname, user) exists in the asset
  inventory.
- Check whether the same alert fired previously (recurring known issue).
- If confirmed false positive: close with "False Positive" disposition
  and document the reason.

Step 2 — Enrich the Alert:
- Resolve hostnames to IPs and vice versa.
- Look up source IPs in threat intelligence (VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB).
- Identify the asset owner and business criticality.
- Check whether the asset has a maintenance window or change ticket.
- Review the affected user's account status and role.

Step 3 — Assign Initial Severity:
- Apply the SOC Severity Policy to set initial severity (LOW/MEDIUM/HIGH/CRITICAL).
- Adjust severity based on asset criticality (crown jewels: +1 level).
- If uncertainty exists, assign higher severity (err on the side of caution).

Step 4 — Initial Containment Decision:
- LOW/MEDIUM: Document, monitor, schedule investigation.
- HIGH: Begin active investigation, consider interim containment
  (block IP at firewall if confirmed malicious).
- CRITICAL: Immediate response per relevant playbook; notify Tier 2
  within 15 minutes.

Step 5 — Document and Track:
- Create a ticket in the SIEM/SOAR/ticketing system immediately.
- Record all enrichment data, actions taken, and decisions.
- Update ticket status throughout the lifecycle.
- Close with one of: True Positive, False Positive, Benign Positive,
  Undetermined.

CLASSIFICATION DEFINITIONS:
- True Positive (TP): Alert correctly identified a real threat.
- False Positive (FP): Alert fired on legitimate/benign activity.
- Benign Positive (BP): Alert fired correctly but activity was authorized.
- Undetermined (UN): Insufficient data to classify; requires further review.

FALSE POSITIVE MANAGEMENT:
- FP rate > 20% for any single rule: submit rule tuning request.
- Document all FPs in the false positive register with the alert rule ID.
- Monthly review of top FP-generating rules with SIEM team.

ALERT QUEUE MANAGEMENT:
- Tier 1 SLA for first review:
  CRITICAL: 15 minutes | HIGH: 1 hour | MEDIUM: 4 hours | LOW: 24 hours.
- Maximum queue depth per analyst: 20 active tickets.
- Unreviewed CRITICAL alerts auto-escalate to shift lead after 15 minutes.
