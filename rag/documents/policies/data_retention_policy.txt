SOC DATA RETENTION POLICY

PURPOSE:
Define how long security data is retained, where it is stored,
and when it is deleted, in support of incident investigation,
compliance, and audit requirements.

RETENTION PERIODS:

Raw Security Logs (Firewall, Proxy, DNS, DHCP):
- Hot storage (SIEM, searchable online): 90 days.
- Cold storage (compressed archive): 12 months.
- Total retention: 13 months.
- Justification: Covers most incident investigation windows and
  satisfies common compliance requirements (PCI-DSS, ISO 27001).

Endpoint Logs (Windows Event Logs, Sysmon, EDR telemetry):
- Hot storage: 30 days.
- Cold storage: 12 months.
- Total retention: 13 months.

Authentication Logs (AD, LDAP, VPN, SSH, RDP):
- Hot storage: 90 days.
- Cold storage: 24 months.
- Total retention: 27 months.
- Justification: Account activity must be retained for longer periods
  for HR, legal, and insider threat investigations.

Security Alerts (SIEM alerts, IDS/IPS events):
- Active incidents: retained indefinitely until closed.
- Closed TRUE POSITIVE incidents: 3 years.
- Closed FALSE POSITIVE / BENIGN incidents: 12 months.

Incident Reports and Case Files:
- Confirmed security incidents: 5 years.
- Near-misses / low-severity closed cases: 2 years.

Network Traffic (PCAP):
- Full packet capture: 7 days (high-storage cost).
- Flow data (NetFlow/IPFIX): 90 days.

Vulnerability Scan Results:
- Most recent 12 scans or 12 months (whichever is longer).

Forensic Evidence (disk images, memory dumps):
- Active investigations: retained until case closure + 6 months.
- Confirmed criminal incidents: consult Legal for extended retention.

STORAGE REQUIREMENTS:
- All archived data must be encrypted at rest (AES-256 minimum).
- Backups of archived data must be verified quarterly.
- Access to archived data requires Tier 2+ authorization and logging.

DATA DELETION:
- Data past retention period is deleted within 30 days of expiry.
- Deletion must be documented and signed off by the SOC Manager.
- Secure deletion (DoD 5220.22-M or equivalent) required for all media.

LEGAL HOLD:
- Any data subject to ongoing legal proceedings or regulatory
  investigation is placed on Legal Hold and exempt from deletion
  until released by Legal Counsel.
