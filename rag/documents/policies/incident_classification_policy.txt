SOC INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION POLICY

PURPOSE:
Provide a consistent taxonomy for classifying security incidents,
enabling accurate reporting, trend analysis, and resource allocation.

INCIDENT CATEGORIES:

CAT-01: Unauthorized Access
- Successful brute force / credential theft.
- Account takeover (ATO).
- Unauthorized physical access to systems.
- Use of stolen credentials.

CAT-02: Malware / Ransomware
- Virus, trojan, backdoor, or rootkit detected and executed.
- Ransomware encryption activity.
- Potentially unwanted application (PUA) with impact.
- Fileless malware / living-off-the-land attacks.

CAT-03: Denial of Service
- Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) against external services.
- Internal resource exhaustion caused by attack.
- Service unavailability caused by malicious activity.

CAT-04: Data Breach / Exfiltration
- Confirmed unauthorized data transfer to external party.
- Loss of physical media containing sensitive data.
- Unauthorized access to PII / PHI / financial records.

CAT-05: Web Application Attack
- SQL injection, XSS, CSRF exploitation.
- Web shell upload and execution.
- API abuse / broken authentication on web APIs.

CAT-06: Insider Threat
- Intentional policy violation with malicious intent.
- Data theft by current or former employee.
- Privilege abuse by authorized user.

CAT-07: Vulnerability Exploitation
- Exploitation of known CVE on network-accessible service.
- Zero-day exploitation.
- Exploitation of misconfiguration.

CAT-08: Social Engineering
- Phishing, spear-phishing, vishing.
- Business Email Compromise (BEC).
- Pretexting or impersonation attacks.

CAT-09: Network Intrusion
- Unauthorized access via network vulnerability.
- Lateral movement between network segments.
- Command & Control (C2) traffic detected.

CAT-10: Policy Violation (Non-malicious)
- Accidental data sharing.
- Unauthorized software installation.
- Use of personal devices for company data without authorization.

CLASSIFICATION PROCESS:
1. Initial classification is assigned at ticket creation by Tier 1.
2. Classification may be updated as investigation progresses.
3. Final classification is confirmed at ticket closure.
4. All CRITICAL incidents must have a formal incident report citing
   the category within 24 hours of closure.

REPORTING:
- Monthly: Incident counts by category, severity, and business unit.
- Quarterly: Trend analysis, top attack vectors, MTTD/MTTR metrics.
- Annually: Full SOC performance report for management.
