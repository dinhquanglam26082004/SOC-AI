T1673 - Virtual Machine Discovery

Tactic: Discovery
Platform: esxi, linux, macos, windows

Description:
An adversary may attempt to enumerate running virtual machines (VMs) after
gaining access to a host or hypervisor. For example, adversaries may enumerate
a list of VMs on an ESXi hypervisor using a Hypervisor CLI such as `esxcli` or
`vim-cmd` (e.g. `esxcli vm process list or vim-cmd vmsvc/getallvms`).
Adversaries may also directly leverage a graphical user interface, such as
VMware vCenter, in order to view virtual machines on a host.   Adversaries may
use the information from Virtual Machine Discovery during discovery to shape
follow-on behaviors. Subsequently discovered VMs may be leveraged for follow-
on activities such as Service Stop or Data Encrypted for Impact.

Detection:
Monitor for anomalous behavior related to this technique.

Mitigation:
  - Follow vendor hardening guidelines.
  - Apply least-privilege principle.
