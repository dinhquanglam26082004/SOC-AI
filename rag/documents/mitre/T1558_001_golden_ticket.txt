T1558.001 - Golden Ticket

Tactic: Credential Access
Platform: windows

Description:
Adversaries who have the KRBTGT account password hash may forge Kerberos
ticket-granting tickets (TGT), also known as a golden ticket. Golden tickets
enable adversaries to generate authentication material for any account in
Active Directory.   Using a golden ticket, adversaries are then able to
request ticket granting service (TGS) tickets, which enable access to specific
resources. Golden tickets require adversaries to interact with the Key
Distribution Center (KDC) in order to obtain TGS.  The KDC service runs all on
domain controllers that are part of an Active Directory domain. KRBTGT is the
Kerberos Key Distribution Center (KDC) service account and is responsible for
encrypting and signing all Kerberos tickets. The KRBTGT password hash may be
obtained using OS Credential Dumping and privileged access to a domain
controller.

Detection:
Monitor for anomalous behavior related to this technique.

Mitigation:
  - Privileged Account Management
  - Active Directory Configuration
