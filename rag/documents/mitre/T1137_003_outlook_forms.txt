T1137.003 - Outlook Forms

Tactic: Persistence
Platform: office suite, windows

Description:
Adversaries may abuse Microsoft Outlook forms to obtain persistence on a
compromised system. Outlook forms are used as templates for presentation and
functionality in Outlook messages. Custom Outlook forms can be created that
will execute code when a specifically crafted email is sent by an adversary
utilizing the same custom Outlook form.  Once malicious forms have been added
to the user’s mailbox, they will be loaded when Outlook is started. Malicious
forms will execute when an adversary sends a specifically crafted email to the
user.

Detection:
Monitor for anomalous behavior related to this technique.

Mitigation:
  - Behavior Prevention on Endpoint
  - Update Software
