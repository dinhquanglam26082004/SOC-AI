T1217 - Browser Information Discovery

Tactic: Discovery
Platform: linux, macos, windows

Description:
Adversaries may enumerate information about browsers to learn more about
compromised environments. Data saved by browsers (such as bookmarks, accounts,
and browsing history) may reveal a variety of personal information about users
(e.g., banking sites, relationships/interests, social media, etc.) as well as
details about internal network resources such as servers, tools/dashboards, or
other related infrastructure.  Browser information may also highlight
additional targets after an adversary has access to valid credentials,
especially Credentials In Files associated with logins cached by a browser.
Specific storage locations vary based on platform and/or application, but
browser information is typically stored in local files and databases (e.g.,
`%APPDATA%/Google/Chrome`).

Detection:
Monitor for anomalous behavior related to this technique.

Mitigation:
  - Follow vendor hardening guidelines.
  - Apply least-privilege principle.
