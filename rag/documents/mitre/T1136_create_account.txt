T1136 - Create Account

Tactic: Persistence
Platform: containers, esxi, iaas, identity provider, linux, macos, network devices, office suite, saas, windows

Description:
Adversaries may create an account to maintain access to victim systems. With a
sufficient level of access, creating such accounts may be used to establish
secondary credentialed access that do not require persistent remote access
tools to be deployed on the system.  Accounts may be created on the local
system or within a domain or cloud tenant. In cloud environments, adversaries
may create accounts that only have access to specific services, which can
reduce the chance of detection.

Sub-techniques:
  T1136.001 - Local Account
  T1136.002 - Domain Account
  T1136.003 - Cloud Account

Detection:
Monitor for anomalous behavior related to this technique.

Mitigation:
  - Operating System Configuration
  - Network Segmentation
  - Privileged Account Management
  - Multi-factor Authentication
