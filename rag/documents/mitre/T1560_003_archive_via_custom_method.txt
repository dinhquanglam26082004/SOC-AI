T1560.003 - Archive via Custom Method

Tactic: Collection
Platform: linux, macos, windows

Description:
An adversary may compress or encrypt data that is collected prior to
exfiltration using a custom method. Adversaries may choose to use custom
archival methods, such as encryption with XOR or stream ciphers implemented
with no external library or utility references. Custom implementations of
well-known compression algorithms have also been used.

Detection:
Monitor for anomalous behavior related to this technique.

Mitigation:
  - Follow vendor hardening guidelines.
  - Apply least-privilege principle.
